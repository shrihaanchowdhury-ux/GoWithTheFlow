A Message From the Founder

Hello to the users of Go With The Flow! My name is Shrihaan Chowdhury, I am 14 years old, and I am a freshman at Northview High School in Atlanta, GA. This is my message to you all, and I hope you guys understand my story.

I have been stuttering from the age of four, and let me tell you, it was really challenging for me to attempt to talk like my peers. I always was thinking and reading so fast, so fast that my mouth couldn't keep up! My words would end up tumbling down like a boulder going down a mountain, and I would end up being totally embarrassed and ashamed of even talking in the first place.

I was and am still such a passionate person, and when I get a bit overexcited, my speech tends to become messy, which ended up being a huge factor of why my confidence levels dropped, and why I couldn't express myself as much as I wanted to. Whenever I tripped up on my words, sometimes kids around me might have laughed at it and then forgot about it like it meant nothing, but deep down, it really hurt my self-esteem and confidence, which led to me becoming hesitant when speaking. I know you all might have also experienced this, and don't worry, I totally get it.

I always regret the missed opportunities where I could have talked to new people, perhaps made new friends, but luckily, I had a great group of friends that I still am friends with to this day, and they continue to make me feel included and welcome. I realized this as I kept meeting new people; don't overthink it, trust me. Just go with the flow.

That is why I decided to make a web app called Go With The Flow, designed to be a platform for all people around the globe who have speech difficulties to improve their speaking skills with the help of my AI chatbot, Flowi, and connect with others online to practice becoming future advocates in online Socratic seminars or have discussions and make genuine connections in the Speak to the World feature.

My love for computer science, robotics and innovation sparked a whole world of passion for me, so now I carry myself with confidence and use my voice to make the impact I want to make in the world, and now it's your turn! Join the Flow! 🌊💙
